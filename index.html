Who we are

Jesus for Life Full Gospel Church (JEFLO) is a member of the Full Gospel Churches of Uganda and is located in Busikiri village, Gombe Sub-County, Wakiso District. JEFLO is 19 miles from the heart of Kampala.

The church started in 2011 as a small community outreach to institutions of learning, hospitals, and prisons with a prayer cell group, and has since been growing in number and spirit. JEFLO has a well-built church structure and a well-laid administrative system committed to share the gospel of Jesus Christ through teaching, prayer, care, and action while encouraging believers to become more like Christ.

Are you strong in faith? Curious about faith, or feel like falling apart? You are welcome!

A present need

A Sunday meal

Many people here can hardly afford two meals a day. Children, the elderly, and single mothers are the most affected. We long to offer a Sunday meal at church — food, and a place to hear the Word of God among friends.
