Who we are

Jesus for Life Full Gospel Church (JEFLO) is a member of the Full Gospel Churches of Uganda and is located in Busikiri village, Gombe Sub-County, Wakiso District. JEFLO is 19 miles from the heart of Kampala.

The church started in 2011 as a small community outreach to institutions of learning, hospitals, and prisons with a prayer cell group, and has since been growing in number and spirit. JEFLO has a well-built church structure and a well-laid administrative system committed to share the gospel of Jesus Christ through teaching, prayer, care, and action while encouraging believers to become more like Christ.

Are you strong in faith? Curious about faith, or feel like falling apart? You are welcome!

A present need

A Sunday meal

In this rural area, many people can hardly afford two meals a day. Some have one. Some go a full day without a complete meal. Children, the elderly, and single mothers are the most affected.

The church already encourages members to work, to share with those who have less, and to welcome help from well-wishers. We also long to feed part of the congregation every Sunday — especially children, the elderly, and single mothers.

A meal at church is more than food. People still walk long distances to find something to eat. If we can offer a Sunday meal, we also invite people to come, hear the Word of God, and sit among friends.

If you would like to partner with this, please write to us on Facebook. May the Lord bless you.
